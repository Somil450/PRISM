## 1.1 Dataset and Forensic Preprocessing (The 7 Pillars)

### Pillar 1: Datasets and Sources
* **FaceForensics++ (FF++)**: Created by Technical University of Munich (TUM). Contains 1,000 pristine YouTube videos and 4,000 manipulated sequences across four algorithms: Deepfakes (autoencoder face-swap), Face2Face (expression reenactment), FaceSwap (graphics-based swap), and NeuralTextures (neural rendering). Evaluated at light compression (C23, H.264) and heavy quantization (C40).
* **Celeb-DF v2**: Created by State University of New York at Buffalo. Features 590 real celebrity interview videos and 5,639 high-resolution deepfakes generated using modern deep generative pipelines with minimized visual boundary artifacts.
* **DF-Platter**: Created by IIIT-Delhi to address demographic bias in multimedia forensics. Features 400 video clips of Indian subjects across Fitzpatrick skin phototypes IV-VI, diverse facial hair (moustaches, beards), regional headwear (turbans, dupattas), and non-studio lighting.
* **ASVspoof 2021 Logical Access (LA)**: Standard international acoustic anti-spoofing benchmark containing 2,500 speech samples generated by state-of-the-art neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, FastSpeech2) and voice conversion toolchains.

### Pillar 2: Number of Samples & Partition Distribution
* **Total Benchmark Scale**: 6,557 video sequences, 509,914 extracted video frames, and 2,500 synthetic speech utterances.
* **FaceForensics++**: 1,000 source sequences (720 train / 140 validation / 140 test).
* **Celeb-DF v2**: Official held-out test partition of 518 videos (340 manipulated, 178 authentic).
* **DF-Platter**: 400 video sequences (280 train / 60 validation / 60 test identities).
* **ASVspoof 2021 LA**: Official 2,500-utterance evaluation partition.

### Pillar 3: Extracted Features & Modality Attributes
* **Visual RGB Patches**: Aligned $[3, 224, 224]$ face crops divided into 196 non-overlapping $16 \times 16$ spatial tokens.
* **PRNU Sensor Noise Residuals**: Single-channel high-frequency sensor pattern noise $[1, 224, 224]$ extracted via a 4-level wavelet / Laplacian filter.
* **Optical Flow Vector Fields**: Dense Farnebäck inter-frame velocity vectors $(\Delta x, \Delta y)$ tracking temporal facial acceleration.
* **3D Surface Normals**: Geometric spatial vectors $(n_x, n_y, n_z)$ tracking cheek, nose, and forehead lighting angles.
* **Corneal Specular Glints**: Pixel coordinates, intensity centroids, and geometric radii of reflections in both pupils.
* **Sub-Dermal Chrominance Pulse**: Melanin-suppressed chrominance projection $S(t)$ over a 32-frame sliding window.
* **Lip-Sync Apertures**: Vertical Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) sequences paired with audio energy waveforms.
* **Acoustic Cepstra**: 80-bin Linear Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (LFCC) and 64-band log-mel STFT spectrograms.
* **Container Atoms**: ISO/IEC 14496-12 box trees (`ftyp`, `moov`, `mdat`) and EXIF metadata tags.

### Pillar 4: Classes and Target Categories
* **Binary Ground Truth**: Authentic (Pristine) vs. Manipulated (Deepfake).
* **Sub-Categories**:
  1. *Identity Replacement*: FaceSwap, DeepFaceLab.
  2. *Expression Reenactment*: Face2Face, NeuralTextures.
  3. *Audio-Driven Lip Synthesis*: Wav2Lip.
  4. *Acoustic Voice Cloning*: HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, ElevenLabs.

### Pillar 5: Data Collection & Sourcing Method
* Academic datasets were downloaded from official research consortia repositories under academic agreements.
* **Real-World Social Media Transcoding Cohort**: 50 test videos (25 authentic, 25 DeepFaceLab swaps) were transmitted through WhatsApp and Telegram, subjecting them to real-world lossy H.264 compression (CRF 32) and container metadata stripping.

### Pillar 6: Forensic Preprocessing Pipeline
1. **Keyframe Sampling**: Video demuxed at uniform 25 FPS; keyframes extracted based on inter-frame histogram differences.
2. **Face Detection & 30% Margin Expansion**: Faces detected using MTCNN / RetinaFace. The bounding box is expanded by **30% along all margins** to preserve the blending boundary where the synthetic face meets the real donor head.
3. **Five-Point Affine Alignment**: Crops aligned using 5 landmarks (pupils, nose tip, mouth corners) and normalized to $[3, 224, 224]$ using ImageNet parameters ($\mu = [0.485, 0.456, 0.406], \sigma = [0.229, 0.224, 0.225]$).
4. **Dual Audio Demuxing**: Native 44.1/48 kHz demuxed for high-frequency vocoder cutoff analysis; 16 kHz mono PCM resampled for LFCC extraction.

### Pillar 7: Training / Validation / Testing Split Protocol
* **Strict Zero-Identity Leakage**: Subjects in the training set are strictly excluded from the test and validation sets.
* FaceForensics++: 720 training videos (72%), 140 validation videos (14%), 140 testing videos (14%).
* Celeb-DF v2: Evaluated on the official held-out test partition of 518 videos.
* DF-Platter: 280 training identities (70%), 60 validation identities (15%), 60 testing identities (15%).

# SECTION 4: The Deep Learning Model: Deep Dive

### 4.1 What Is the Model and Where Does It Live?
* **Class**: `PRISMNetwork` in [`src/model.py`](file:///D:/PRISM/PRISM-Provenance-Reasoning-Intelligence-System-for-Multimedia/src/model.py).
* **Adapter**: `DoRALinear` in [`src/dora_layer.py`](file:///D:/PRISM/PRISM-Provenance-Reasoning-Intelligence-System-for-Multimedia/src/dora_layer.py).

### 4.2 The Five Internal Building Blocks
1. **ViT-B/16 Backbone (`self.vit`)**:
   * Uses `vit_base_patch16_224` from `timm`.
   * Splits the $224 \times 224$ RGB image into 196 non-overlapping patches ($16 \times 16$ pixels).
   * Generates 196 visual tokens $[B, 196, 768]$ plus 1 CLS token $[B, 1, 768]$.
2. **DoRA Adapter Injection (`inject_dora`)**:
   * Base weights $W_0$ (86 million parameters) are completely frozen.
   * Replaces every linear layer with `DoRALinear`. Trains only rank-8 matrices ($A$ and $B$) and a learned magnitude vector $m$, totaling **1.2 million trainable parameters**.
3. **Sensor Noise Encoder (`self.noise_enc`)**:
   * A 4-stage convolutional network that takes the $224 \times 224$ single-channel PRNU noise residual and reduces it via stride-2 convolutions:
     $$224 \to 112 \to 56 \to 28 \to 14$$
   * Projects the $512 \times 14 \times 14$ feature map into 196 noise tokens $[B, 196, 768]$.
4. **Cross-Modal Multi-Head Attention (`self.fusion`)**:
   * Multi-head attention (8 heads, 768 dimensions).
   * Visual tokens act as Query ($Q$); Noise tokens act as Key ($K$) and Value ($V$).
   * Correlates facial pixels with sensor noise tokens before classification.
5. **Spatial Mask Decoder (`self.mask_dec`)**:
   * Takes the 196 fused tokens, reshapes them to $[B, 768, 14, 14]$, reduces channels to 256, and upsamples through 4 transposed convolutions:
     $$14 \to 28 \to 56 \to 112 \to 224$$
   * A final $1 \times 1$ convolution with `Sigmoid()` outputs the $[B, 1, 224, 224]$ tampering probability map.

### 4.3 Why This Architecture? (Why This Only?)
* **Why ViT over CNNs**: CNNs rely on local high-frequency textures that get completely destroyed by WhatsApp compression (CRF 32). ViT's global self-attention compares distant facial regions (e.g. left eye vs. right eye, neck vs. chin) that survive aggressive compression.
* **Why PRNU Sensor Noise**: Physical camera sensors have microscopic silicon imperfections (PRNU). AI generators (GANs, diffusion) render images purely in software and lack physical silicon noise. The dual-stream checks for the presence of this real-world hardware signature.
* **Why DoRA over LoRA**: Standard LoRA couples directional and magnitude updates. DoRA decouples them, achieving the representation capacity of full fine-tuning while training only 1.2M parameters and running under **1,450 MB VRAM** on an RTX 3050 Laptop GPU.

### Agent 1: Container & C2PA Metadata Agent

* **Forensic Rationale**: Automated deepfake pipelines leave signatures like `Lavf` (Libavformat) or mobile deepfake toolchains (`FaceApp`, `CapCut`, `InShot`, `ByteDance`). Conversely, authentic cameras embed hardware manufacturer tags, lens parameters, and cryptographic C2PA provenance manifests.
* **Context-Aware Tiered Scoring**:
  * AI generator keywords in text chunks (e.g., Stable Diffusion, Midjourney) -> $m(T) = 0.99$.

# SECTION 6: Dempster-Shafer Evidential Fusion & Graph Reasoning

Implemented in [`src/fusion.py`](file:///D:/PRISM/PRISM-Provenance-Reasoning-Intelligence-System-for-Multimedia/src/fusion.py), PRISM fuses evidence over the frame of discernment $\Omega = \{\text{Tampered } (T), \text{Authentic } (A)\}$.

### 1. Basic Belief Assignment (BBA)
Every agent outputs masses that sum to $1.0$:
$$m(T) + m(A) + m(U) = 1.0$$
where $m(U)$ represents **epistemic uncertainty** (uncommitted belief).

### 2. Dempster's Rule of Combination
When combining two independent masses $m_1$ and $m_2$:
$$m_{1,2}(C) = \frac{\sum_{X \cap Y = C} m_1(X) m_2(Y)}{1 - K}$$
where $K$ is the **conflict factor**:
$$K = m_1(T) m_2(A) + m_1(A) m_2(T)$$
* If two agents agree, $K \to 0$.
* If one agent says 100% Tampered and another says 100% Authentic, $K \to 1.0$. If $K > 0.70$, PRISM raises a **High Conflict Alert** for human analyst review.
* **Total Conflict Singularity Handling ($K \ge 0.999$)**: Standard Dempster's rule is undefined at $K = 1.0$ due to division by zero. Rather than masking contradiction with an artificial cap, PRISM detects total conflict ($K \ge 0.999$) and returns pure epistemic uncertainty ($m_U = 1.0$), correctly identifying complete evidentiary contradiction.
* **The Missing Modality Rule**: If an agent is missing (e.g. silent video, $m(U) = 1.0$), it acts as the mathematical identity element:
  $$m_{1,2}(T) = \frac{m_1(T) \cdot 1.0}{1 - 0} = m_1(T)$$
  **It leaves all visual evidence completely undiluted!**

### 3. Dynamic Forensic Evidence Graph
Agents are connected based on physical dependencies:
* `("vit", "temporal")`: Visual appearance agrees with motion velocity.
* `("comp", "vit")`: JPEG compression error boundary aligns with ViT patch mask.
* `("prnu", "comp")`: Camera sensor noise loss correlates with DCT recompression grid.
* `("audio", "lip")`: Acoustic speech energy correlates with lip aperture.
* `("gaze", "rppg")`: Biological ocular glints correlate with biological blood pulse.

Connected nodes that agree receive an **Exponential Evidentiary Coupling Boost**:
$$w = 1.0 + \gamma \cdot e^{-\beta \cdot (u_U + v_U)}$$
where $\gamma = 1.5, \beta = 2.0$. High-uncertainty nodes naturally decouple, while high-confidence physical observations cross-corroborate.

### 4. Forensic Non-Annihilation Rule (Zadeh's Paradox Resolution)
In criminalistics, a single definitive smoking gun (e.g., an explicit deepfake metadata tag, a vocoder 16 kHz brickwall cutoff, or a C2PA certificate failure with $m(T) \ge 0.72$) must **never be outvoted by circumstantial clues**. PRISM enforces the exact lower bound without arbitrary offset:
$$\text{BetP}(T) \ge \max_{i \in \mathcal{H}} m_i(T)$$

### 5. High Epistemic Conflict Protection (SWGDE Standard)
If the ViT backbone strongly certifies authenticity ($m(A) \ge 0.65, m(T) \le 0.15$), accidental physical noise (like camera shake or monitor glare) will **not** trigger a false conviction. The system declares **INCONCLUSIVE** rather than falsely accusing an authentic video.

### 6. Pignistic Probability Decision Rule
$$\text{BetP}(T) = m(T) + 0.5 \cdot m(U)$$
* $\text{BetP}(T) \ge 0.65 \implies$ **TAMPERED (DEEPFAKE)**
* $\text{BetP}(T) \le 0.35 \implies$ **AUTHENTIC (REAL)**
* $0.35 < \text{BetP}(T) < 0.65 \implies$ **INCONCLUSIVE** (sent to expert examiner).

# SECTION 8: Viva & Defense Cheat Sheet

### Q1: "What is the difference between your model and your agents?"
> *"The model (`PRISMNetwork` in `src/model.py`) is a deep learning Vision Transformer that inspects visual tokens and physical camera PRNU noise on the GPU, outputting a spatial tampering heatmap. The 8 agents (in `src/agents/`) are specialized forensic micro-services running on the CPU that check specific physical, biological, and acoustic properties (like heart rate, eye reflections, JPEG error levels, and audio vocoder frequencies). Their outputs are fused using Dempster-Shafer evidential reasoning."*

### Q2: "Why did you use DoRA instead of standard LoRA or full fine-tuning?"
> *"A full Vision Transformer has 86 million parameters, requiring 16 GB of VRAM. Standard LoRA couples directional updates with magnitude updates, causing unstable training on small deepfake datasets. We implemented DoRA (Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation in `src/dora_layer.py`), which mathematically decouples weight magnitude from direction. This freezes 98.6% of base weights and trains only 1.2M parameters, allowing the entire model to run in 41.9 ms (23.9 FPS) using only 1,450 MB of VRAM on our RTX 3050 Laptop GPU."*

### Q3: "Why use Dempster-Shafer Theory instead of simple probability averaging or Softmax?"
> *"Simple averaging cannot handle missing data. If an investigator examines a silent video without an audio track, a standard machine learning model assigns a neutral probability of 0.50 to the audio channel, which lowers the overall score and dilutes valid visual tampering evidence. Under Dempster-Shafer theory, our acoustic agents assign a mass of 1.0 to epistemic uncertainty ($m(U) = 1.0$), which acts as the mathematical identity element under Dempster's rule, leaving the visual evidence completely undiluted."*

### Q4: "How does your system handle diverse demographic groups and skin tones?"
> *"Traditional remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) uses only the green color channel, which fails on darker skin tones (Fitzpatrick types IV-VI) because melanin absorbs green light, triggering false alarms. In our rPPG agent (`src/agents/rppg_agent.py`), we implemented a chrominance-based (CHROM) projection that projects skin color changes perpendicular to the melanin absorption vector. When evaluated on the DF-Platter Indian demographic dataset, our system achieved 93.8% accuracy without demographic bias."*

### Q5: "How do you ensure court admissibility?"
> *"We adhere directly to NIST SP 800-86. Upon ingestion, we generate SHA-256 and BLAKE3 cryptographic hashes to establish a tamper-proof chain of custody. Every agent outputs an explainable physical rationale rather than an opaque black-box score, and the system can export a complete, signed forensic dossier in PDF and JSON formats."*
