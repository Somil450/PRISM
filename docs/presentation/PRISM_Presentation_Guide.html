1. Proposed Project Architecture

The PRISM pipeline operates across four strictly partitioned stages to guarantee scalability, low VRAM footprint, and court-admissible explainability.

📦 Stage 1: Preprocessing & Routing

Unpacks video containers, extracts 16 uniform keyframes, captures adjacent frame bursts for motion flow, extracts 16 kHz mono audio via FFmpeg pipe, and strips container EXIF metadata.

👁️ Stage 2: Dual-Stream ViT Backbone

Combines a ViT-B/16 Vision Transformer with DoRA (Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation) and a Laplacian PRNU sensor noise residual stream. Tunes only 1.2M parameters.

🕵️ Stage 3: Multi-Agent Ensemble

8 independent domain experts inspect container headers, JPEG compression grids (ELA), Farneback optical flow jerk, 3D illumination vectors, pupil gaze, cardiac rPPG pulses, and voice formants.

⚖️ Stage 4: Evidential Fusion & Dossier

Dempster-Shafer evidence theory resolves multi-agent conflicts, isolates epistemic uncertainty m(U), and outputs a 4-section court-admissible forensic report and a 224x224 pixel forgery heatmap.

Validation Split & Anti-Leakage Protocol

• Split Strategy: 50/50 Identity-Disjoint Partitioning ($N=100$ balanced evaluation cohort: 50 authentic, 50 tampered).
• Zero Identity Leakage: Training and testing subjects never share identity, preventing facial memorization shortcuts.
• Zero Metadata Leakage: All agents execute _validate_no_leakage_kwargs() - if any caller passes filename, path, or label, the code immediately crashes with a ValueError.

3. Implementation of Proposed Work & Working Demo

The 8 Specialized Forensic Detectives (How They Work in Code)

1. Metadata Agent

Code: src/agents/metadata_agent.py
Parses EXIF, XMP, C2PA. Detects generative software tags (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion). WhatsApp stripped metadata is safely marked as Uncertainty m(U)=1.0, not fake.

2. Compression (ELA) Agent

Code: src/agents/compression_watermark_agent.py
Re-compresses image at 95% JPEG quality. Spliced face-swaps display dramatic difference in compression error rates (Error Level Analysis) and 8x8 DCT grid misalignment.

3. Video Temporal Agent

Code: src/agents/video_temporal_agent.py
Computes Farneback dense optical flow and second-order kinematic jerk ($j_t$). Detects static screen recordings ($<0.20$ px/f) to prevent false jitter conviction.

4. 3D Illumination Agent

Code: src/agents/illumination_agent.py
Estimates 3D surface normals. Computes angular divergence between facial light vectors and background ambient lighting. Disables when no human faces are present.

5. Eye Gaze Agent

Code: src/agents/eye_gaze_agent.py
Tracks pupil center vectors. AI autoencoders often render eyes independently, resulting in strabismus (mismatched directional rays) or impossible corneal reflections.

6. Biological rPPG Agent

Code: src/agents/rppg_agent.py
Tracks subtle green-channel skin color pulses (540 nm) caused by cardiac blood pumping. Real humans show a clear heart rate peak (45-180 BPM); deepfakes have a flat spectrum.

7. Lip-Sync Agent

Code: src/agents/lip_sync_agent.py
Measures normalized cross-correlation between vertical mouth opening distance and acoustic audio energy envelope. Detects audio-visual dubbing lag anomalies.

8. Audio Voice Agent

Code: src/agents/audio_voice_agent.py
Scans STFT Mel-spectrogram for neural vocoder brickwall cutoffs (sharp frequency drop above 16 kHz) and robotic pitch formant continuity characteristic of ElevenLabs clones.

Dempster-Shafer "Honest Judge" Fusion (The Core Logic)

Why Dempster-Shafer Solves Real-World Problems:

In standard AI, probability must sum to 1.0: $P(\text{Fake}) + P(\text{Real}) = 1.0$. If a video is compressed, standard AI is forced to guess.
In Dempster-Shafer, mass sums over three possibilities: $$\mathbf{m(Tampered) + m(Authentic) + m(Uncertainty) = 1.0}$$ When WhatsApp strips metadata, the Metadata Agent allocates mass to m(Uncertainty) = 1.0 instead of falsely accusing the video of being fake.

6. Teacher Viva Cheat-Sheet (Top Questions & Winning Answers)

❓ Q1: "Where are the 100 test samples and how did you get 95% accuracy?"

Your Answer: "Sir, in data/cache/perturbation_grid/ we created 105 systematically degraded samples cataloged in perturbation_features.csv. We tested against 10 real-world conditions like WhatsApp compression (CRF 35-45), 50% downscaling, noise, and blur. Out of 100 balanced test samples (50 real, 50 fake), 47 fakes were caught and 48 real samples were cleared, giving exactly 95.00% accuracy. The baseline single model only got 78.2% on the same corrupted set."

❓ Q2: "Why use Dempster-Shafer instead of standard Softmax average?"

Your Answer: "Sir, Softmax is forced to split 100% of its belief between Fake and Real. If a video is compressed, Softmax flips a coin and makes a false accusation. Dempster-Shafer introduces a third state: Uncertainty m(U). When metadata or sensor noise is missing due to WhatsApp, mass goes to Uncertainty rather than falsely convicting an innocent video."

❓ Q3: "What is DoRA and why did you choose it over LoRA?"

Your Answer: "Sir, LoRA couples update direction and magnitude together. DoRA decouples the weight matrix into its direction vector and a separate scalar magnitude. This mirrors full model fine-tuning while freezing 98.6% of the 86M ViT weights. It tunes only 1.2M parameters and runs in under 1.5 GB VRAM on our RTX 3050 GPU."

❓ Q4: "How does the rPPG heartbeat agent work?"

Your Answer: "Sir, human hemoglobin absorbs green light at 540 nm. Every time the heart pumps, facial skin color changes microscopically. We extract the green channel over time and compute a Fast Fourier Transform. Real humans show a clear heart rate peak between 45 and 180 BPM (0.75-3.0 Hz). Deepfake faces lack biological blood flow and produce a flat, noisy spectrum."

❓ Q5: "How did you solve the screen recording false alarm?"

Your Answer: "Sir, cameras have physical silicon noise (PRNU), but computer screen recordings have zero sensor noise. Standard filters flag zero noise as AI smoothing. We debugged this by adding physical logic: if our primary ViT neural model verifies that facial skin features are authentic ($P < 0.25$), zero noise is correctly recognized as a computer screen capture rather than an AI deepfake."
